## Hi there 🧭

I'm a French cybersecurity engineer with a background in **Linux, networks, infrastructure and IT/OT environments**.

My work is increasingly focused on **Offensive Security Research**, particularly **OSINT-driven reconnaissance, External Attack Surface Management (EASM) and Attack Path Analysis**.

I also have a background in private investigation and OSINT. What I enjoy most is piecing together information that doesn't seem connected at first, until a clearer picture starts to emerge.

I approach security by trying to understand how a system actually works: what it is there for, who relies on it, what can be discovered from the outside, and how access and permissions turn into the ability to act.

## What I focus on

**External attack surface & OSINT**  
Discovering assets and exposures, tracing relationships and being careful about what public evidence really establishes.

**Attack paths & security architecture**  
Understanding trust boundaries, identities, delegated access, effective privileges and how an attacker might move from one capability to another.

**IT/OT & cyber-physical security**  
Examining remote administration, operational constraints, safeguards, recovery and the potential consequences for physical systems and the people who depend on them.

These interests are supported by years of hands-on work with infrastructure, Linux, networks, security operations and industrial environments. I still build labs because sometimes the best way to understand a design is to see how it behaves.

## Projects and labs

### [PRE-ATT&CK Human Surface](https://github.com/lxrider/pre-attack-human-surface)

What can someone learn about a privileged person before ever interacting with their systems? A defensive OSINT project exploring online exposure, reconnaissance and ways to reduce unnecessary clues and connections.

### [SaaS Security Lab](https://github.com/lxrider/saas-security-lab)

A multi-tenant SaaS, **RedRocket Engage**, built to explore how business requirements become trust boundaries, authorization decisions and concrete application security problems.

*Work in progress.*

### [AI Exposure Lab](https://github.com/lxrider/ai-exposure-lab)

Research and practical exercises around generative AI exposure, business use cases, cognitive and action delegation, and the limits of the authority granted to users and agents.

*Work in progress.*

### [Linux Platform Engineering](https://github.com/lxrider/linux-platform-engineering)

A hands-on lab built around KVM, RHEL 9 and automation, covering virtualization, provisioning, administration, hardening and reproducible environments.

### [RHEL Golden Image](https://github.com/lxrider/rhel-golden-image)

A reproducible RHEL 9 reference image built with Kickstart, focusing on unattended installation, secure defaults and repeatable deployment.

### Security Architecture Lab

Ongoing experiments with segmentation, identity, firewalling, IT/OT trust boundaries, logging and detection. I use these environments to test architectural choices and understand the trade-offs behind them.

## How I work

**Start with the problem.** I first want to understand the business, the environment and the people who need the system to keep working.

**Connect the evidence.** A small technical detail, a public record or an unexpected relationship may change the whole interpretation. I try to distinguish observations, documented capabilities and hypotheses rather than force everything into a neat story.

**Get hands-on when it helps.** I've learned a lot from building systems, breaking things in authorized labs and fixing services under real operational pressure. Sometimes that meant being alone in a datacenter at night with a critical service to restore. Those experiences stay with you.

**Make the result useful.** A finding matters when someone can understand it, verify it and decide what to do next.
